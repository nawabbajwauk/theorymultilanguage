Simple privacy promise

Learning stays free. Learner data should be treated with respect.

This app asks for a name and email or phone only when a learner chooses to register free access or receive learning updates.

What is collected

Name, contact detail, preferred goal, selected language, consent status and registration time.

Why it is collected

To keep a record of free learners, send useful learning updates, and improve the service.

What is not allowed

Learner contact details should not be sold. Learners should not be spammed.

Removing details

Before launch, add your contact email here so learners can ask to remove or update their details.
